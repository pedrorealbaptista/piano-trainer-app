Step one

Finding C

  1. Ignore the white keys and look only at the black keys. They come in groups: 2, then 3, then 2, then 3, all the way along.
  2. Find any group of 2 black keys.
  3. The white key directly to the left of that pair is C. Every pair of black keys has a C just before it.
  4. From C, the white keys go C D E F G A B, then C again.
